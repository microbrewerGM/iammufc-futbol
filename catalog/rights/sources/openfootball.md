# ToS snapshot — openfootball

- **Source:** `https://github.com/openfootball` (notably `eng-england`)
- **Captured:** 2026-08-18
- **Captured by:** human, transcribed from the project research set
  (`starting_ideas/01-data-sources-and-licensing.md`)
- **Capture method:** manual. **Never fetched by an agent.**

## Position

**Public domain.** Community-maintained football fixtures and results published
without restriction.

## Assessment

`redistributable: true`. Zero licensing constraint — the only source in the
manifest with nothing to reason about.

Used as a cross-check against Football-Data.co.uk rather than as a primary
source. Two independent public-domain results sets disagreeing is a useful
data-quality signal, and it costs nothing to have both.

## Caveat

Community-maintained means accuracy varies and is not guaranteed by anyone.
Treat disagreements with Football-Data.co.uk as something to investigate, not
as one source being authoritative over the other.
